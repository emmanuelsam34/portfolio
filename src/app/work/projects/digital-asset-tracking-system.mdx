## Overview

The **Digital Asset Tracking System (DATS)** is an enterprise asset lifecycle and custody management system built on **Frappe and ERPNext**. It replaced a vulnerable, paper/PDF-based equipment allocation routine with an auditable system of record that tracks hardware from initial inventory load through assignment, double-signed handovers, repair cycles, reassignment, and final retirement.

Rather than building a disconnected custom tracker, the solution intentionally builds upon native ERPNext DocTypes (`Asset` and `Asset Movement`). This design preserves standard depreciation, reporting, and accounting links while introducing the governance features Frappe lacks out of the box: **captured digital signatures (in-person canvas and remote portal)**, **orthogonal custody-versus-condition state machines**, and **tamper-evident approval workflows**.

## Core Architectural Innovations

### 1. Orthogonal Dual-Status Modeling

Traditional asset registers frequently conflate *custody* (who is responsible) with *condition* (operational health). When a laptop is sent for repair, naive systems reassign it to "Maintenance", erasing the accountable custodian.

DATS decouples these concerns into three independent dimensions:

| Dimension | Managed On | Definition & Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Asset Custody Status** | `Asset` (Durable) | Defines accountability: `Unassigned`, `Pending Assignment`, `Assigned`, `Pending Return`, `Returned`, `Pending Reassignment`, `Lost/Missing (stores)`, `Retired/Disposed`. |
| **Asset Condition Flag** | `Asset` (Durable) | Defines physical state: `In Service`, `Under Repair`, `Lost while assigned`. Updated via repair orders or inspection flags without altering custody. |
| **Movement Approval State** | `Asset Movement` (Transient) | Tracks the active handover paperwork: `Draft` → `Pending Approval` → `Pending Release` → `Pending Receipt` → `Completed` / `Rejected`. |

An asset can exist as *"Assigned to John Doe / Under Repair"*—preserving custodian accountability even while physical hardware sits with external repair vendors.

### 2. The Three Signed Movement Workflows

Every custody transition maps to an immutable, state-gated `Asset Movement` document:

1. **Issue (First Assignment)**:
   - Initiated by the Project Coordinator → Approved by PMO Lead → Signed upon receipt by Employee.
   - Custody transitions to `Assigned`.
2. **Return (Employee Offboarding)**:
   - Outgoing Custodian A signs physical release and records condition → Receiver/IT inspects and signs receipt.
   - Dual signatures eliminate disputes over post-return damage. Custody transitions to `Returned → Unassigned`.
3. **Reassignment (Direct Transfer A → B)**:
   - Custodian A signs release → Custodian B inspects and signs acceptance.
   - Custody transfers directly to B upon completion.

---

### 3. Departed-Custodian Release Bypass

Offboarding workflows frequently deadlock when an exiting employee separates without signing asset release forms.

To prevent orphan inventory without faking signatures:
- The system provides a **PMO Lead Release Bypass** (`custom_release_bypass = 1`).
- The PMO Lead executes release on behalf of the departed employee with a mandatory, auditable justification note (e.g., *"Custodian separated; asset recovered by department manager"*).
- The incoming receiver (or stores inspector) still signs normally, ensuring the receipt half of the audit trail remains intact.

---

### 4. Operational Guardrails & Defensive Controls

- **Single-Asset Integrity Constraint**: Enforces exactly one asset per movement document (`1 movement = 1 asset = 1 person`). Cohort bulk issues are rejected to prevent diluted signature accountability.
- **Post-Release Rejection Recovery**: If an incoming custodian rejects an asset after the outgoing custodian has signed release, custody automatically reverts to Custodian A while the failure is investigated (preventing assets from disappearing into limbo).
- **Concurrency Asset Locking**: An open, unsubmitted movement temporarily locks the asset. Concurrent movement requests or repair tickets are blocked until the active transaction completes or cancels.
- **Charger & Accessory Bundling**: Peripherals (chargers, docks, adapters) are tracked as child accessory lines against the primary serialized laptop record, preventing micro-movement clutter while holding custodians accountable for bundled hardware.
- **Immutability of Submitted Records**: Upon final completion (`docstatus = 1`), movement records become strictly read-only across all roles. Corrections require explicit, auditable reversing movements.

---

### 5. Multi-Mode Signature Capture

The signature layer accommodates both physical office handovers and distributed remote teams:
- **In-Person Mode**: Renders an HTML5 drawing canvas directly on the coordinator's device at physical handover.
- **Remote Mode**: Dispatches an automated Workflow Action alert linking the employee directly to their authenticated ERP portal to review specifications, attest condition, and sign digitally.
- Stamped signature images are embedded directly into the immutable document record.

## Technical Stack

- **Platform**: Frappe Framework v14, ERPNext v14
- **Languages**: Python (Server Scripts / Hooks / Validation Engine), JavaScript (Custom Client Scripts / Canvas API)
- **Data & Workflow Primitives**: Custom Fields on `Asset` & `Asset Movement`, DocType Workflows, Notification Engine, Scheduled Background Jobs (3-Day Acknowledgement Daemons)
- **Governance**: Multi-Role RBAC, Dual-Sided Digital Signatures, Immutable Movement Ledgers
