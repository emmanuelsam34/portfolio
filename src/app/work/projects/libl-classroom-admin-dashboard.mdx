## Overview

The LIBL Classroom Admin Dashboard is the operational layer behind the tutoring marketplace. It gives internal teams the controls required to manage tutors, students, bookings, verification, and financial workflows in one place.

## Product Scope

A tutoring marketplace cannot run on customer apps alone. Admin teams need visibility into platform health, user states, booking issues, verification queues, and payout-related workflows. This dashboard provides those controls and helps connect the tutor and student products to day-to-day platform operations.

## Key Features

- Platform KPI and health overview
- Tutor and student management
- Booking and session oversight
- KYC review workflows
- Financial and payout administration
- Content and curriculum management support

## Architecture / Integrations

The dashboard uses React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Radix UI, TanStack Query, Recharts, and Appwrite. That stack supports both rich internal UI composition and the data-heavy workflows needed by an admin team working across operational contexts.

Because it sits at the center of the LIBL platform, the dashboard works as a coordination surface for states created in the mobile apps and verification systems. It reflects the platform reality behind bookings, tutor records, financial actions, and compliance workflows.

## My Focus

My focus here was internal product engineering: building the control layer that makes the rest of the platform manageable. I worked on workflows that support operations teams rather than end users directly, which required clear interfaces, reliable state handling, and practical information design.
