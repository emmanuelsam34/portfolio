## Overview

The KYC Verification Portal is a productized verification workflow for tutors. It replaces a more fragile custom verification approach with a hosted identity flow and a more dependable orchestration model.

## Product Scope

The portal supports a very specific but high-value part of the platform: confirming tutor identity in a way that is easier for users and more trustworthy for the business. It needed to accept tutor context securely, launch a hosted verification session, process verification outcomes, and feed those results back into platform state.

## Key Features

- Verification session orchestration
- Hosted identity flow redirection
- Signed token validation
- Webhook-driven status updates
- Attempt management and verification lifecycle tracking
- Email notifications tied to verification outcomes

## Architecture / Integrations

The implementation uses React, Vite, TypeScript, Appwrite, Didit, Resend, and Vercel serverless functions. The architecture validates verification tokens, creates Didit sessions, receives webhook events, verifies HMAC signatures, and updates tutor records inside Appwrite.

This makes the portal an orchestration layer rather than a simple frontend. Its job is to coordinate a compliance-sensitive workflow across external identity services, backend state, and product-facing outcomes for tutors and admins.

## My Focus

My focus was on making verification more reliable and operationally safer by shifting from a brittle custom pipeline to a dedicated provider-backed flow. That meant designing around trust, request validation, webhook integrity, and clear status transitions instead of just document upload screens.
