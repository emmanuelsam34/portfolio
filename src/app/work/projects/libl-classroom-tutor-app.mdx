## Overview

The LIBL Classroom Tutor App is part of a broader tutoring ecosystem that supports tutors, students, and internal operations through connected product surfaces. This app focuses on the tutor side of the marketplace, giving tutors the tools to manage classes, interact with students, create learning content, and track the operational side of their work.

## Product Scope

The product needed to support more than a simple teaching interface. Tutors needed onboarding, booking management, messaging, live session support, earnings visibility, and pathways into KYC verification for payout eligibility. The app also had to align with the admin dashboard and student app so that workflows stayed coherent across the full platform.

## Key Features

- Tutor onboarding and profile management
- Booking request and schedule handling
- In-app messaging and session coordination
- AI-assisted quiz and assignment generation
- Earnings tracking and payout support
- KYC readiness for tutors who need verification before certain financial actions

## Architecture / Integrations

The app is built with Expo, React Native, TypeScript, and Tamagui, with Appwrite powering backend services and shared platform workflows, plus integrations for Paystack, BigBlueButton live classes, Expo Notifications, and OpenRouter-powered AI generation.

This made the app more than a mobile UI layer. It became a client for a wider system of notifications, session logic, payment-related workflows, and content generation features that had to remain stable across multiple user journeys.

## My Focus

My work centered on building dependable tutor-facing product flows and shaping how those flows connected to shared platform capabilities. That included the experience around bookings, session operations, messaging, payouts, and AI-assisted learning content, while also making sure the mobile experience fit within the wider LIBL ecosystem.
